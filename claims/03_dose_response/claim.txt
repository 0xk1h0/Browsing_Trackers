CLAIM 3 - The more a session moves from one site to another, the more
tracking data it leaks.

Paper
    Section 7.1 and Figure 3.

Run
    bash claims/03_dose_response/run.sh

    About 2 seconds. Bins 9,002 agent sessions from seven agents by their
    cross-site transition count, then regenerates Figure 3.

What you should see
    Per-bin medians, one row per transition bin.

        bin      n   tracker   pseudo IDs   context   device/net   finger
        0     3684       4.0         19.5      79.0        188.0    11.7%
        1     2628      15.5        116.5     277.5        541.5    40.1%
        2      407       6.0        187.0     293.0        590.0    56.8%
        3-5   1311     100.0        306.0     605.0       1397.0    43.4%
        6-10   410      58.0        602.5    1114.0       2497.5    68.3%
        11+    562      62.0        971.0    1903.5       4275.5    54.6%

How this compares to the paper
    The three metrics Figure 3 plots rise from bin to bin with no reversal.
    End points against the numbers Section 7.1 quotes:

        quantity                 paper           this run
        pseudo IDs               20 to 971       19.5 to 971
        context signals          79 to 1,903     79 to 1,903.5
        device/network           191 to 4,275    188 to 4,275.5
        tracker hosts, bin 0     4               4
        tracker hosts, bin 1     15              15.5

    Two values do not match. The device and network floor is 188 here
    against 191 printed. The fingerprint-trigger share is 11.7% and 40.1%
    in the two low bins against 11.2% and 40.8% printed, while the bin 11+
    value of 54.6% matches. Both are recorded in artifact/CLAIMS.md under RQ2.

    The tracker-host column is not monotone, at 4, 15.5, 6, 100, 58, 62.
    Figure 3 does not plot it and the paper does not claim it is monotone.
    The run also prints a per-agent paired summary against a scripted
    same-site baseline, which is a separate analysis.

Checking it yourself
    Diff the two copies of the table, or run python3 artifact/verify.py rq2.
