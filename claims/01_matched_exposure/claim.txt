CLAIM 1 - A browser agent sent to do a task contacts more third-party
tracker hosts than a person doing the same task from the same start page.

Paper
    Section 6.1 and Table 1, "Per-task unique tracker hosts on the matched
    10-task pool". The abstract states the Amazon--32 case.

Run
    bash claims/01_matched_exposure/run.sh

    Under a second. Covers 220 human sessions (22 participants x 10 tasks)
    and 140 agent sessions (7 agents x 10 tasks x 2 repetitions).

What you should see
    Median unique tracker hosts per session. The right column is the range
    across the seven agents.

        task                  human   agents, low to high
        Amazon--32                5   27 to 52
        Apple--29                 2    2 to  8
        ArXiv--17                 0    0 to  9
        Booking--3               32   36 to 66
        Coursera--21             27   10 to 40
        GitHub--35                2    2 to  7
        Google Flights--34        2    5 to 13
        Google Map--35            3    7 to 14
        Google Search--28       116    4 to 26
        Huggingface--14           0    8 to 12
        overall median            3    4 to 11

How this compares to the paper
    All ten human cells match Table 1, including the overall median of 3.
    Of the 70 agent cells, 62 match and 8 differ, the largest being
    UI-TARS on Amazon--32, printed 50 and reproduced 27. Table 1's agent
    overall medians span 6.5 to 11.5 where this run gives 4 to 11, which
    follows from that same UI-TARS column. See artifact/CLAIMS.md,
    corrections C1 and C2.

    Google Search--28 runs against the claim in both the paper and the run,
    because the humans retyped queries across result pages. The paper
    discusses this case, which is why it claims no uniform per-task rise.

Checking it yourself
    Diff the two copies of the table, or run python3 artifact/verify.py rq1
    to compare every cell numerically.

        diff claims/01_matched_exposure/expected/table1_trk_hosts.tsv \
             artifact/rq1_human_vs_agent/expected_outputs/table1_trk_hosts.tsv

    The overall median row is printed to the console only, not to the TSV.
