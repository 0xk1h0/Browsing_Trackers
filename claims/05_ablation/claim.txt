CLAIM 5 - Taking the agent's site-crossing actions away, at both the tool
schema and the proxy, and blocking consent-banner CDNs, sharply cuts how
many identifiers a session transmits.

Paper
    Abstract ("reduces median identifier transmission by 69-76% across the
    benchmark"), Section 8, Table 5 for the condition names, Table 6 for
    the numbers.

Run
    bash claims/05_ablation/run.sh

    About 1 second. Recomputes every ablation cell over 643 tasks per cell,
    two agents and five conditions, 6,430 sessions.

What you should see
    Median pseudonymous identifiers per session.

        condition                       Fara-7B   Browser-Use
        C0 default                          317           346
        C2 no navigate                      122            64
        C5 no back, negative control        320           285
        C7 no navigate, no CMP               98            54
        C8 no search, navigate, CMP         132           105

        C7 reduction against C0           69.1%         84.4%

    Operational success at C0 is 54.6% for Fara-7B, 99.5% for Browser-Use.

How this compares to the paper
    Eight of the ten medians match Table 6. Fara-7B matches on all five
    conditions, its C7 reduction of 69.1% matches the printed -69%, and the
    negative control behaves as described, C5 at 320 against a 317 baseline.

    Two Browser-Use cells do not match. C0 prints 227 where the data gives
    346, and C5 prints 185 where the data gives 285. Because C7 itself
    reproduces at 54, the reduction becomes 84.4% rather than 76%, so the
    abstract's range becomes 69 to 84%. artifact/CLAIMS.md covers the C0
    cell as correction C3. C3 covers both the C0 and the C5 cell.

    Table 6's operational success rates of 89.5% and 36.8% at C0 are not
    derivable from any shipped file. This run gives 54.6% and 99.5% for the
    same two cells. The camera-ready replaces operational success with
    graded WebVoyager task success, as committed in the author response.
    See artifact/CLAIMS.md, correction C4.
